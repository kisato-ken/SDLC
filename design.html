The Two Compounding Links Architecture

Why the SDLC Immune System compounds instead of acting as isolated tooling.

Compounding Link 1 · Drift → Risk

When requirement text edits occur, Trace Agent flags downstream design and test nodes as STALE. Rather than acting as a passive warning label, stale states immediately escalate the requirement's RiskScore in RiskLens (e.g. 2.0 → 6.0/10).

Compounding Link 2 · Rule → Retroactive Drift

When Postmortem-to-Prevention ingests historical incident reports, it extracts reusable security/architectural rules (e.g., AES-256 encryption at rest). The rule is immediately replayed against the entire existing graph, retroactively staling violating designs.
